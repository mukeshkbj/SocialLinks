# Social Links — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 24 November 2026*

**Social Links collects no data.** Nothing you save leaves your browser except through your browser's own sync service, if you have it turned on.

## What the extension stores
The links you save (platform, username or address, and any name you give an Other link), your profile names, and the optional name you put on your share card. They are kept in your browser's extension storage: synced through your browser account (Google, Microsoft, Mozilla or Apple) if you use sync, or kept on your computer only.

## What it does not do
- It does not read or collect anything from the websites you visit. It has no permission to.
- It only changes a page when you choose **Paste here**: it types the chosen link into the text box you right-clicked, in that tab, that one time. It reads nothing from the page.
- It does not read your clipboard. It only writes to it when you choose **Copy**.
- It does not read your browsing history, tabs, cookies or passwords.
- It makes no network requests, contains no analytics or tracking, and loads no remote code.
- It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.

## Optional site icons (Chrome and Edge)
If you click **Show site icons**, the browser lets the extension display icons from its own icon cache for your saved Other links. Nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn it off at any time.

## Your control
You can remove any link, delete profiles, export a backup, or uninstall the extension, which deletes everything it stored on this computer.
